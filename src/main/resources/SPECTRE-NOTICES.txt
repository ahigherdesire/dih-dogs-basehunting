This native DIH sync carries the Dogs 3.0.6 updates to the fork's shared modules,
including finder, palette, activity, Spawner Protect, staff/visibility, home,
rendering and trading adaptations and their required helpers.
It does not bring over Fake Stats, fake payments/roles, donor client frameworks,
authentication, updaters, telemetry or a separate Spectre runtime dependency.
Existing DIH rendering, settings, finder reports and inventory/navigation
coordination are reused. The original source adaptation targeted AUTISM Client.

Modifications include native DIH lifecycle and packet handling, client-thread
queues, bounded caches and scans, cleanup on world changes/disconnect, preserved
DIH addon identifiers, opt-in observation UI and server-confirmed spawner storage.
The source snapshot's tested algorithms are retained with Minecraft 26.2/26.3
adapters. Native geode predictions have explicit structure-free model limits.
